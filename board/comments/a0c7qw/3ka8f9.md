Their board had a forty-line status report inside `resolution`, which
the spec permitted by saying only "Free. Common: fixed, duplicate,
wontfix". Now stated as a short token, with prose belonging in a comment
or the body, and `resolution` outside a complete column called out as
meaningless.

Two validator warnings added and both verified firing: one when
`resolution` appears on an issue that is not in a complete column, one
when its value runs long or spans lines.
